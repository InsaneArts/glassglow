GlassGlow

Light moving through glass, on any SwiftUI view.

View on GitHub

One modifier.

The shape is the view's alpha: an SF Symbol, text, a Shape, an image or a whole composition. The view keeps its layout, and the halo takes no touches.

import GlassGlow

Image(systemName: "heart.fill")
    .resizable()
    .scaledToFit()
    .frame(width: 240, height: 240)
    .glassGlow()

Text("Glow")
    .font(.system(size: 120, weight: .black))
    .glassGlow(.sunset)

Any shape

Circle, RoundedRectangle or your own Shape.

Real glass, in Metal.

A distance field gives the glass its depth. Ten lights move through it, the edge bends and splits them, and a soft halo spills around the shape.

10 lightsTen moving lights, tinted by your palette.
6 s loopSet the speed, glow, refraction and colour split, or freeze a moment.
5 platformsiOS, iPadOS, macOS, tvOS and visionOS.

Install.

Add the package with Swift Package Manager. Requires iOS 17, macOS 14, tvOS 17 or visionOS 1. Without Metal, the view shows unchanged.

.package(url: "https://github.com/InsaneArts/glassglow", from: "0.1.0")
